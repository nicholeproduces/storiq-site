Built for mobile businesses, where nothing is the same but everything is important.

Film and TV props, wardrobe, event gear, rental stock, moving boxes, garage bins. Take a picture of what's in a box, and you'll always know where it is.

  1. 01

    Snap a photo

    Take a photo, choose one from your library, or type the items yourself.

  2. 02

    Review the names

    Check the names before you save them. Change or remove any that are not right.

  3. 03

    Choose your words

    Rename Space, Bin, and Item to the words you use. Jobs stays Jobs.

  4. 04

    Spaces and bins

    Put items in a bin inside a space. Open the bin to see what is in it.

  5. 05

    Search, or just say it

    Type a name, or tap the microphone and say what you are looking for.

  6. 06

    Sell and quote

    Open a quote from the items you already have, or sell one by copying the listing into your marketplace.

  7. 07

    Report a problem

    Open Settings and choose Report a problem. Tell us what went wrong.
