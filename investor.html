MYBOXai · Investor brief

Inventory that starts with a photo.

Live on iPhone and Android

Snap a photo of a bin, box, or shelf and MYBOXai builds a searchable item list, so crews always know what they have and where it is.

View the deck Email us

Hours saved in prep, minutes saved on set.

On a film set, props live in inventories inside inventories: bins inside cases inside trucks. When a new crew member joins, nobody can tell them what's on the truck. Spreadsheets go stale and memory walks out the door.

Behind the scenes, the truck is the warehouse, and it moves to a new location every day. Lighting designers load in while a night crew preps so the day crew can get ahead. Small engine repair shops work the same way: parts in drawers, machines out on a call, and the person who knows where it is is not always the one standing at the truck.

MYBOXai turns a photo into an organized, searchable inventory of spaces, bins, and items, so anyone on the crew can find what they need fast.

Priced the way productions actually buy.

MYBOXai is a subscription for individuals and ongoing teams. But studios and productions often can't run open-ended subscriptions: accounting approves spend per show, and nobody wants a purchasing card left attached to an account after wrap.

Subscription

For individuals and teams with ongoing inventory. Monthly or annual.

In development

Production Pass (in development)

  • One production, set start and end dates
  • Named crew on the pass for those dates
  • One approved payment, nothing to cancel at wrap
  • Show templates, plus pull lists and to-dos department heads send to crew
  • Owner can recover and transfer the database

Built for the people who move from show to show: the leadman or prop master keeps their own inventory in MYBOXai, brings it into each production, and takes it with them when the show wraps.

We're raising $5–10K to build MYBOXai Teams and the Production Pass.

MYBOXai works today for one person. Teams lets crews share one inventory, with roles for who can view and who can edit. The Production Pass packages that for a single show, start to finish. The founder is unpaid; every dollar goes to the work below.

This round funds paid pilots. Next step: a pre-seed raise to fund a full-time team.
