Savage Props LLC · Investor brief

Operational visibility for physical assets.

MyBoxAI is an AI-assisted visual inventory platform that turns physical assets into searchable, actionable records.

We are beyond concept stage: core workflows, cloud infrastructure, and cross-device synchronization are functioning in a small private beta cohort.

We are raising to accelerate execution and launch readiness, not to validate whether the problem exists.

Customer segments

Focused markets with clear pain points.

We are entering where pain is acute and founder context is strongest, then expanding deliberately.

Film and television

Production companies, prop departments, rental houses, studios.

Property operations

Property teams, maintenance groups, facilities, vacation rentals.

Small business

Contractors, photographers, event teams, retail inventory operators.

Institutional

Museums, archives, schools, and university operations.

Enterprise adjacency

Asset operations, equipment tracking, and distributed field teams.

Future expansion

Emergency logistics, municipal equipment, and public works inventory.
